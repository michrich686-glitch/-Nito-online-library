What Nito Library Is

Nito Library is a free online library where anyone can search for books and read them directly in the browser — no sign-up, no downloads. Browse educational books, kids' books, novels, story books, horror, religious texts, and more, right from the menu above.

Every book opens instantly in a full-page reader — flip through the real scanned pages via Internet Archive, or switch to a guaranteed-working full text of public-domain titles via Project Gutenberg. Nito AI, the built-in chat assistant, is on hand to help you understand what you're reading.
